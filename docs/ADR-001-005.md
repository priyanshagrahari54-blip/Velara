# Architecture Decision Records

## ADR-001 — RaycastVehicle + force-at-CoM discipline
**Context:** Hero car ko real suspension chahiye (drift/jumps), wheels as
colliders heavy + jittery hote hain.
**Decision:** cannon-es `RaycastVehicle`; engine split 42% front / 58% rear.
**Lesson (bug story):** `applyForce(force, worldPoint)` cannon-es mein
**relative** point leta hai — galat se `body.position` pass karne par ~100m
lever arm ban gaya → 30 km/h cross hote hi car rocket-launch. Fix: force at
CoM (no point). Rule: gameplay forces hamesha CoM pe, kabhi world-point se.
**Consequence:** suspension tuning per-mass (stiffness 160 @ 950 kg).

## ADR-002 — Ground = big BOX, not infinite Plane
**Context:** RaycastVehicle wheels Plane ground pe ~5.5s tak traction lose
karte the (stuck creep).
**Decision:** Static Box(700,1,700) top at y=0. Node test se verified.
**Consequence:** Map bound 520m pe soft-reset (game.js world-bounds guard).

## ADR-003 — Guaranteed-clear spawn blocks
**Context:** Player random building ke andar spawn hua → deep penetration →
ground ke andar phans gaya (y=-1.26).
**Decision:** Center block (2,2) = spawn plaza (fountain), block (4,2) =
stunt plaza — dono building-free. Road-side hi pickups/props.
**Consequence:** Naye POI bhi guaranteed-clear zones mein hi.

## ADR-004 — Traffic = dynamic servo, not kinematic
**Context:** Kinematic (infinite mass) AI cars hero car ko orbit mein fling
kar deti thi; hero unke upar chadh ke phas jaata tha.
**Decision:** mass 1500 + fixedRotation + velocity servo (k=dt·2) + predictive
braking (1.2s look-ahead) + 3.5m any-direction full stop. Hero↔traffic contact
material: friction 0.2, restitution 0.05.
**Consequence:** Accidents ab bhi hote hain (GTA-feel) par finite aur
recoverable.

## ADR-005 — Speed governor + blow-up clamps
**Context:** Constant engine force + no drag → unlimited speed → tunneling
through ground (test mein 412 km/h, y=-6.7!).
**Decision:** (1) Governor: >185 km/h forward → engine cut. (2) linearDamping
0.03. (3) Velocity hard-clamp 55 m/s. (4) Angular clamp 7 rad/s.
(5) World-bounds soft reset. (6) Perched/flipped auto-recover (2s).
**Consequence:** Physics kabhi "paagal" nahi hota — arcade-chaotic par bounded.
