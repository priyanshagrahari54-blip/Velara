VELARA — City of Shadows (v0.2, test build)
============================================

CHALANE KA TARIKA (Windows 7 SP1 / 8 / 10 / 11):
  1. Is folder ko kahin bhi extract karo (e.g. Desktop).
  2. VELARA.exe double-click karo.
  3. PLAY dabao — khelna shuru!

AGAR BLACK SCREEN / ERROR AAYE:
  - Purane laptop GPUs (jaise Lenovo G560 ka Intel HD) par hardware WebGL2
    missing ho sakta hai. App khud "safe mode" (software graphics) mein
    restart kar leti hai — 2-3 second wait karo.
  - Manual safe mode: VELARA.exe par right-click > Create shortcut,
    shortcut Properties > Target ke end mein space ke saath likho:  --safe

SLOW CHAL RAHA HAI?
  - Game ke andar K dabao — graphics quality cycle hoti hai
    (HIGH -> MEDIUM -> LOW). Purane laptops par LOW best hai.

CONTROLS:
  WASD chalna/gadi | Shift daud | Space jump/handbrake | E gadi mein baitho
  Mouse camera | H horn | N din-raat | R barish | C camera | T gadi theek | Esc pause

Note: Ye Electron par bana hai (Chromium 108). Electron ka license folder
mein LICENSE.electron.txt hai. Game 100% offline hai — koi internet nahi chahiye.
